# Privacy Policy

**Garmin to ZWO Converter** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## Data Collection

This extension operates entirely locally within your browser. When you use the extension:

- Workout data is read directly from the Garmin Connect website
- The data is processed locally to generate a `.zwo` file
- No data is sent to any external server
- No cookies, local storage, or persistent data are used

## Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions to function:

- `host_permissions`: Access to `connect.garmin.com` to read workout data from the page and inject the download button

No data leaves your browser.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/tsueri/garmin-zwo-converter).
